Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the initial setup take?+

Setup is complete in under 5 minutes. Installing the lightweight CloudForge host agent requires a single terminal command and doesn't require server restarts.

Can we migrate our existing clusters with zero downtime?+

Absolutely. Our agent runs parallel to your existing stack during configuration, so your workload transitions smoothly with zero interruption.

How secure is the CloudForge agent?+

CloudForge utilizes a read-only telemetry architecture for standard monitoring. It is strictly secured over encrypted TLS endpoints, keeping private keys completely isolated.

What clouds are supported out of the box?+

AWS, GCP, and Azure have deep native console integration. We also provide full custom support for raw bare-metal servers, DigitalOcean, and Linode.

Is there an auto-scaling threshold limit?+

No strict limits. Pro plans are configured with defensive bounds to prevent runaway bills, while Enterprise tiers support infinite nodes.
